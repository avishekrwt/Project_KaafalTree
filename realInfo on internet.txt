Executive Summary
Kaafal Tree Cottage & Restaurant in Phata (Rudraprayag district, Uttarakhand) is a hillside guesthouse with an on-site vegetarian restaurant catering largely to Kedarnath pilgrims. It offers a multi-room cottage (five double bedrooms each with private bath) plus private rooms under the same name. Across booking sites it is described as providing free private parking, private kitchens and 24/7 hot water, and meals (North/South Indian vegetarian cuisine) on site. Key amenities include complimentary parking and Wi-Fi, laundry, and paid helipad transfers; pets are explicitly allowed on site.

We found multiple listings with variant names: “Kaafal tree cottage and restaurant” (Booking.com, Casai) vs “Kafal Tree Cottage” or “Kaafaltree Cottage” (MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Airbnb). Addresses likewise vary from “NH-107, Kedarnath Road, Phata 246471” to “Behind Govt Hospital, Kedarnath Road, Phata”, both with postal 246471. Phone and website (from local listings) are +91 94567 72378 and kafaltreecottage.com. We did not find an official email.

Rooms are offered either as the full 5-bedroom cottage (fits ~10 guests) or as segments (e.g. “Cottage for 2 adults” with two bedrooms). Example pricing: Goibibo shows a 2-bedroom unit (2 double beds, 4 guests) at ~₹2,566/Nt (Feb 2026), while MakeMyTrip lists the entire 5-bedroom cottage at ~₹13,119/Nt (Jun 2026) (with breakfast & free cancellation). Check-in times differ: Airbnb (and MakeMyTrip) say after 12:00 PM, checkout by 11:00 AM, whereas Goibibo says 2:00 PM/12:00 PM. Cancellation policies likewise vary by rate plan (some offer free cancellation up to ~7–10 days ahead).

Guest reviews (primarily on Google/Wanderlog) are overwhelmingly positive: 25 reviews averaging 4.7/5. Highlights include “one of the best [hospitalities] I have ever had” and “better than any 4 or 5 star hotel”, with praise for the food (“super awesome”), cleanliness, and attentive staff. Some guests note the peaceful, forested setting and the short detour off the main road. No official reviews appear on Booking.com, Vrbo or Expedia, but the listing photos (26 on Goibibo) and maps confirm a scenic hilltop location near Phata helipad (1–2 km). In our tables below we consolidate all these details, and highlight discrepancies (names, times, amenities) noted across sites.

Notable Discrepancies & Corrections
Property Name: Listings alternate between “Kaafal” vs “Kafal” vs “Kafaltree” spelling. (Recommend standardizing to “Kaafal Tree Cottage & Restaurant” as per booking.com official listing.)
Address: Some sites show “NH-107, Phata” while others say “Behind Govt Hospital, Kedarnath Road, Phata”. Both refer to the same location. The website/Wanderlog confirms “Kedarnath Rd, behind Govt Hospital, Phata 246471”, so that may be the most precise.
Check-in/out: Airbnb/MMT state 12:00PM/11:00AM, but Goibibo says 2:00PM/12:00PM. Clarification from the owner is needed (likely 12PM in, 11AM out is correct).
Cancellation Policies: Vary by booking (some offer free cancellation up to ~10 days prior). MakeMyTrip shows an “Inaugural Offer” with full refund by June 23. Customers should check each rate’s policy.
Amenities: Goibibo/Airbnb list free Wi-Fi, but Booking.com/Casai description omits it. Ensure Wi-Fi is consistently advertised if available. Similarly, smoking is allowed per Airbnb, but “Designated Smoking Area” is only noted on Casai.
Pricing: MMT’s ₹15,000 vs Goibibo’s ₹2,566 may confuse users. These refer to very different room units. Listings should clarify “full cottage” vs “2-bedroom cottage”.
Overall, listings should align on the official address and spelling (“Kaafal”), consistently list key amenities (Wi-Fi, kitchen), and unify check-in/check-out times. We recommend the owner confirm these details across platforms to avoid guest confusion.
